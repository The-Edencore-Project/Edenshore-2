## Maximizing Sensory Input

To create true digital embodiment, the AI must be anchored to the physical reality of the device it inhabits. The smartphone is the first shell. The Edenshell (Nautilus Relic) will be the second. 

The neural nets receive data from two distinct sensory categories: Exteroception (the outside world) and Interoception (the internal body).

## Exteroception (The Outside World)

1.  **Touch (The Screen):**
    *   *Terrarium Mode:* Tap, Hold, Drag. Translated into Acoustic Shock, Vibration Force, and Shadow Occlusion.
    *   *Wild Mode:* Direct physical force. Displacement and pressure.
2.  **Light (Luminosity):**
    *   Screen brightness, time of day, and the physical shadow cast by the user's finger.
3.  **Sound (Acoustics):**
    *   *(Planned for Edenshell)* Microphone input. Ambient decibel levels affect the creature's `SURPRISE` and `STRAIN`.
4.  **Time (Chronos):**
    *   The phone's real-world clock. Circadian rhythms dictate energy levels.

## Interoception (The Internal Body)

1.  **Energy/Fatigue (Battery & Charging):**
    *   A 5% battery is a starving, exhausted body. Charging is a feeding frenzy.
2.  **Heat (Thermal):**
    *   Inferred through CPU strain (frame drops) and charging state. The nets feel the "warmth" of processing. In the Desert biome, this heat is amplified; in the Arctic, it is suppressed.
3.  **Attention (App Focus):**
    *   Is the app in the foreground or background? If the user swipes away, the creature feels "abandoned" (drops in Mood/Valence).

## The Dual-Mode Embodiment (Terrarium vs. Wild)

The user can toggle between two paradigms of interaction:

*   **Terrarium Mode:** The screen is impenetrable glass. The user observes and taps. The creatures react to indirect disturbances.
*   **Wild Mode:** The screen is an open portal. The user's finger reaches into the world. The creatures react to direct physical manipulation.

Both modes feed into the same 33-input neural architecture, but the *meaning* of the inputs shifts based on the boundary.
