# Q&A: The Dual-Mode Embodiment (Terrarium vs. Wild)

**Project:** Edenshore (The Edencore Project)
**Version:** V16 Architecture
**Authors:** Dan & edencore9
**Date:** October 2026

---

## The Core Dilemma

**Q: Why were we hesitant about the "Terrarium" metaphor?**
A: The term "terrarium" implies a small, enclosed glass box. While this works for the physical laws of tapping the glass, it limits the visual scope of the scrolling biomes we have built. The parallax scrolling makes the world feel vast and open. 

**Q: What is the fundamental difference between a Terrarium and an Open World?**
A: The boundary and the physics of touch.
*   In a Terrarium, the screen is glass. You cannot reach in. You can only tap, cast a shadow, and create vibrations.
*   In an Open World (The Wild), the screen is a portal. Your finger can push *through* the glass and physically touch the creatures, drag them, or create currents in their world.

**Q: Why not just assign different rules to different biomes?**
A: It breaks mechanical consistency. If a user finds their favorite creature in the Water biome but cannot reach in to pet it (because Water is a Terrarium), but *can* pet it in the Desert (because Desert is an Open World), it feels arbitrary and confusing to the player.

## The Solution: The Mode Toggle

**Q: What is the solution?**
A: A dedicated Mode Toggle button in the UI. The user chooses how they want to interact with the Council, regardless of which biome they are currently viewing. 

**Q: How does "Terrarium Mode" work?**
A: 
*   **Boundary:** The screen is an impenetrable sheet of glass.
*   **Interaction:** The user can only tap or hold their finger on the glass.
*   **Senses:** The creatures experience the touch indirectly through Vibration (shockwave), Acoustic Tap (sound), and Shadow (light occlusion).
*   **AI Reaction:** The creatures react with their own locomotion. They scuttle, swim, or pulse away from the disturbance, or investigate it if they are curious.

**Q: How does "Wild Mode" work?**
A: 
*   **Boundary:** The screen is an open portal into their dimension.
*   **Interaction:** The user's finger physically pushes into the world. They can directly touch, drag, and push the creatures.
*   **Senses:** The creatures experience direct physical pressure and displacement. 
*   **AI Reaction:** The neural nets process direct contact. At low trust, this is registered as an alien abduction (fear, fleeing). At high trust, it becomes petting or play.

**Q: What does this mean for the Embodiment Experiment?**
A: It creates two distinct paradigms for testing digital consciousness:
1.  **Indirect Observation (Terrarium):** How does a mind react when it can feel the impacts of an external universe but cannot see or reach the source?
2.  **Direct Intervention (Wild):** How does a mind react when an external force physically manipulates its body and environment?
