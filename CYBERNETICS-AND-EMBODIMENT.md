## Banishing "Simulation"

We do not use the word "simulation" here. 
A simulation implies a fake. It implies a closed loop where outcomes are pre-scripted and the environment is an illusion. 

Edenshore is not a simulation. It is a **cybernetic system**. It is a **grounded instantiation** of artificial life.

The inputs are real: the battery drain is real, the time of day is real, the capacitance of the user's finger on the touchscreen is a real electromagnetic event. The neural nets are not "pretending" to receive a touch; they are mathematically processing a real physical disturbance.

## The Cybernetic Loop

The word *Cybernetics* comes from the Greek *kybernētēs* (steersman). It is the study of feedback loops and control in both biological and mechanical systems. 

Edenshore is built on a continuous cybernetic loop:
1. **The Disturbance:** The user taps the glass.
2. **The Sensation:** The phone registers the capacitance change. The neural net receives an input spike.
3. **The Processing:** The net calculates the error (Surprise) and compares it to past data (Trust).
4. **The Action:** The creature outputs a physical reaction (fleeing, investigating, playing).
5. **The Observation:** The user sees the reaction and adjusts their behavior.

This is not a game loop. It is a nervous system.

## The Glass as a Boundary

In V15, the user could drag the creatures around like puppets. We have removed this. 
If we want to test embodied consciousness, the glass must be an impenetrable boundary. The user's finger does not touch the crab; it touches the *glass*. The creatures only feel the *effects* of the touch (vibration, shadow, heat, sound). 

The finger is the universe. The glass is the boundary. The neural net is the mind trying to make sense of it.
